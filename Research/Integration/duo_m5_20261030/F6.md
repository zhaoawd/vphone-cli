## KFC app exits at launch: X1 accessibility bundle (2026-10-31)

Evidence `.build/acceptance/cases/kfc-crash-20261031T103702Z/`. On `duo-candidate-20261030` (`ea05ba81`) the KFC
app (`com.yum.kfc.brand.prod.enterprise` 6.39.0, plain `arm64`) disappeared
within seconds of launch, leaving `SUPER_APP_Prod-2026-10-31-183705.ips`.

- The report's exception is `EXC_GUARD` / `GUARD_TYPE_MACH_PORT` `MOD_REFS`,
  on thread 22, the app's own crash reporter (`YUMCCrash Exception Handler
  (Primary)`, in `mach_port_deallocate`). That is a symptom, not the cause.
- The main thread was in `+[AXSpringBoardHomePopGlue initialize]`, loaded by
  `UIAccessibilityLoader` from
  `/System/Library/AccessibilityBundles/SpringBoardHomePopAccessibility.axbundle`.
  That bundle is `arm64e.x1`, is loaded from disk (not from the shared cache),
  and the faulting PC is offset `0xc58`, whose first instruction is
  `pacibsppc` (ESR `0x02000000`, unknown/undefined instruction). The bundle
  holds 66 PAuth_LR/CPA instructions.
- Cause: on-disk X1 images rely on launchd's on-demand lowering service,
  whose exception port is inherited through spawn. KFC installs its own Mach
  exception ports at startup, so the `pacibsppc` fault goes to its
  reporter instead of the lowering service, and the reporter then trips the
  port guard. Apps without their own handler (GeoBare, relaunched in the same
  state) survive because the service lowers the instruction.
- Trigger: the accessibility bundles load only while app accessibility is on.
  At the time `com.apple.Accessibility` had `ApplicationAccessibilityEnabled`
  and `AutomationEnabled` = 1. vphoned's `ui.tree`/`ui.tap_element` turn
  these on only for one call and restore them if they changed them, so
  what left them on is not established. This session had used those calls
  (network/audio checks), and earlier F2 runs did not.
- Confirmation: after setting both to 0 and rebooting the guest, KFC launched,
  stayed frontmost with the same PID for 20 s, showed its home page
  (`kfc-ax-off.jpg`) and wrote no report.
- Not a regression of this integration's display/fold work; the same gap
  applies to any app with its own Mach exception handler that runs non-cache
  X1 code. Possible fixes, not done: statically lower the X1 instructions in
  `/System/Library/AccessibilityBundles` (as
  `system-cibarcode-cfw-arm64e_x1` does for CIBarcode), or forward faults
  from a replaced exception port. Workaround for app checks: keep app
  accessibility off and do not use `ui.*` RPCs while such an app is
  launching.
